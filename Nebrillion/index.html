HOW TO PLAY

You take the handoff at your own 25. You get 7 plays to punch it into the end zone.

Every play is a question about Nebraska football over the last three seasons (2023–2025): who carried the ball, who led in tackles, how many games the Huskers won, how they did against ranked teams and more. You have 25 seconds on each one.

The faster you answer, the farther you run. A wrong guess costs 3 seconds, but you get a hint: higher, lower, more wins, fewer losses. If the clock hits zero, you're sacked.

Type records like 7-6. Type numbers as digits or words. Last names work for players.
