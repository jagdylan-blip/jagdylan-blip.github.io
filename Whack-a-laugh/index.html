At the Tomb

    About the Tomb

    The Tomb of the Unknown Soldier sits on a hill at Arlington National Cemetery, overlooking Washington, D.C. Its west face reads: “Here rests in honored glory an American soldier known but to God.”

    Soldiers of the 3rd U.S. Infantry Regiment — “The Old Guard” — have guarded it every minute of every day since 1937, in every kind of weather. Signs on the plaza ask visitors for silence and respect, and when a crowd forgets, the Sentinel will stop the walk and remind them.

    This game is a lighthearted look at that duty. The real place is a solemn one: if you visit, stay quiet and stay behind the chains.

    Sources: Arlington National Cemetery; 3rd U.S. Infantry Regiment (The Old Guard).
